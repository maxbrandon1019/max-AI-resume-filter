Senior Backend Distributed Systems Engineer

About the Role:
We are seeking a Senior Backend Engineer to design, build, and scale resilient distributed backend systems and high-concurrency event-driven architectures.

Key Responsibilities:
- Design high-performance distributed systems using Python (FastAPI/Django) and Go.
- Architect event-driven asynchronous pipelines with Apache Kafka and RabbitMQ.
- Model and optimize high-throughput relational and NoSQL databases (PostgreSQL, Redis, Cassandra).
- Partner with infrastructure teams to deploy and operate services on Kubernetes.
- Lead code reviews, promote engineering excellence, and mentor junior engineers.

Requirements:
- 6+ years of backend software engineering experience.
- Expert-level proficiency in Python and/or Go (Golang).
- Strong background with SQL/PostgreSQL performance tuning, indexing, and Redis caching.
- Production experience with message brokers like Apache Kafka.
- Hands-on experience with Docker, microservices architectures, and Linux.
- Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science or related field.
